# Name check: is "Talo" safe to build on?

A first screen, done before buying a domain, creating permanent accounts or launching commercially. It is **not legal clearance**: before money goes into the brand, a trademark lawyer should confirm the result.

Countries: **United Kingdom, United States, Nigeria** (where Talo will sell or the company will register).

Results are recorded privately in the progress notes, not in this public file. This file only says what to check and how to decide.

---

## 1. What we search

| Term | Why |
|---|---|
| TALO | The exact name |
| TALLO, TALOW, TAALO | Sounds the same |
| TALOS | Looks and sounds close, and is used for "Talos Intelligence" (Cisco), which sits near our "intelligence" positioning |
| TALO + partner / intelligence / AI | The name used the way we'd use it |

## 2. Trademark registers (all free)

Look for **live** marks (registered or pending, not dead or expired) in these classes:

| Class | Covers |
|---|---|
| 9 | Downloadable software |
| 35 | Business consulting, marketing, business intelligence |
| 42 | SaaS, software as a service, online platforms |

| Where | Register | How |
|---|---|---|
| Many countries at once | WIPO Global Brand Database (branddb.wipo.int) | Search the terms; filter by the UK and US. Good first pass. |
| United States | USPTO Trademark Search (tmsearch.uspto.gov) | Search wordmarks and similar spellings. Check owner, status, classes and goods description. |
| United Kingdom | UK IPO "Search for a trade mark" (trademarks.ipo.gov.uk) | Also check EU marks that still cover the UK as "comparable" UK marks. |
| Nigeria | Trademarks, Patents and Designs Registry (iponigeria.com) | Nigeria isn't covered by international (Madrid) registrations, so it needs its own search. Online records may be incomplete; note anything that can't be checked online. |

## 3. Same name in the market

Search for "Talo" in software, AI, business development, partnerships, sales and intelligence:
- Google (first three pages), Product Hunt, Crunchbase, LinkedIn companies, G2.
- Apple App Store and Google Play.
- GitHub organisations.

Record any company using the name: what it does, where, and how big it looks.

Already known: **talo.com** and **talo.club** are in commercial use.

## 4. Domains and handles

Check, without buying anything:
- Domains: talo.ai, talo.io, talo.so, gettalo.com, usetalo.com, talohq.com, trytalo.com, talo.partners.
- Handles: X, GitHub, LinkedIn page, Telegram.

Only record something as "available" if a registrar or the platform itself shows it as available on the date checked. Prices for premium domains are noted, not paid.

## 5. How we decide

| Result | Meaning | What we do |
|---|---|---|
| 🔴 **Rename** | A live TALO (or near-identical) mark in class 9, 35 or 42 in the UK, US or Nigeria, **or** a known company using the name for business, sales or partnership software | Pick a new name before anything public. |
| 🟡 **Proceed carefully** | The name exists only in unrelated fields (food, fashion, games, etc.), or the conflicts are dead marks | Keep building. Choose a distinctive domain and get a trademark lawyer's check before the commercial launch. |
| 🟢 **Clear** | Nothing relevant found | Keep building. A lawyer's check is still advised before filing our own mark. |

Dan makes the final call.
